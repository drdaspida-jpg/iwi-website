Our Commitment

Empowering People. Strengthening Families. Building Resilient Communities.

We are a women-led, non-governmental and non-religious organization working from the grassroots to promote women's rights, education, health, economic empowerment, peacebuilding, food security and social protection.

Rebuilding the Broken Wall

Our signature programme focuses on rebuilding families, restoring relationships, strengthening individuals and creating peaceful, healthy and economically resilient communities.

Learn More About IWI
Our Vision

A flourishing and empowered society

A society where women, girls, children and other vulnerable groups enjoy equal access to opportunities, resources and rights, championed by supportive male advocates for inclusive and sustainable development.

Our Mission

Strengthening community development

To strengthen community development through impactful programmes that empower women, girls, children, men and other vulnerable groups through economic empowerment, healthcare, education and social support, while promoting violence-free spaces for all.

What We Do

Programmes built around community needs

Education

Education

Supporting vulnerable and grassroots children to access education and remain in school.

Empowerment

Women's Economic Empowerment

Helping women develop skills, strengthen livelihoods and become more self-reliant.

Health

Health & Wellbeing

Promoting health awareness, access to services, reproductive health, nutrition and WASH.

Protection

Women's Rights & Protection

Promoting women's rights, safe spaces and protection from violence and abuse.
