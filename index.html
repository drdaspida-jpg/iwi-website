Our Mission

Every woman deserves the tools to build the life she chooses.

To strengthen community development by delivering impactful programs that empower women, girls, children, men, and other vulnerable groups through economic empowerment, access to health care, education, and social support—while actively promoting violence-free spaces for all

Community-Led

Every program is designed with the women who will use it.

Radically Transparent

Full financial reporting, published annually.

Long-Term

We commit to communities for years, not single campaigns.

Locally Staffed

96% of our field team lives in the communities we serve.
