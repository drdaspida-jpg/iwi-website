Creating Change from the Grassroots

Our Impact

Impact means more than numbers.

It means seeing a child return to school, a woman gain economic independence, a family rebuild trust, a young person find purpose and a community become safer and more resilient.

Women Empowered

Knowledge, skills and opportunity

Women and girls gain knowledge, skills, confidence and opportunities to improve their lives.

Children Supported

Education and life skills

Vulnerable children receive support to access education, develop life skills and pursue their dreams.

Families Strengthened

Peace, dialogue and support

Families are supported through peacebuilding, dialogue, psychosocial support and community engagement.

Young People Equipped

Mentorship and livelihoods

Youth receive mentorship, leadership development and livelihood opportunities.

Communities Mobilized

Grassroots participation

Community leaders and members participate in creating solutions to social, health and economic challenges.

Systems Influenced

Evidence and advocacy

Through advocacy, partnerships and evidence, we contribute to improved policies, services and community responses.

Our Approach to Impact

From community voice to sustainable change

Advocacy

Amplifying evidence and community priorities.

Bringing people and stakeholders together.

Capacity Building

Building knowledge, skills and leadership.

Service Delivery

Empowerment

Creating pathways to dignity and self-reliance.

Research & Learning

Using evidence to improve programmes and influence change.

Our goal is to ensure that interventions are community-owned, evidence-informed and sustainable.
