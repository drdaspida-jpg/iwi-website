Four pillars, built to compound.

Education

Scholarships & Literacy

Full and partial scholarships from secondary school through vocational certification, paired with mentorship. Our adult literacy classes run in the evenings so working women can attend without giving up income.

2025 reach: 2,100 students · 340 adult learners

Economic Empowerment

Skills & Micro-Enterprise

Vocational training in trades with strong local demand, seed grants for new businesses, and peer savings circles that provide capital and accountability without predatory interest.

2025 reach: 1,860 women · 410 businesses launched

Health

Maternal & Community Health

Mobile clinics bring prenatal, maternal and general health services to areas without reliable access, alongside community health-worker training and reproductive health education.

2025 reach: 3,400 patients · 28 mobile clinic routes

Leadership

Voice & Leadership Academy

A structured track in public speaking, civic process and organizational management for women pursuing local leadership and community-board roles.

2025 reach: 260 fellows · 40 now in public roles

How It Works

From enrollment to independence

Step 1

Community intake

Local advisory councils identify need and nominate participants.

Step 2

Program placement

Participants are matched to the pillar and track that fits their goals.

Step 3

Active support

Training, mentorship and check-ins over the full program cycle.

Step 4

Graduation & follow-up

We track outcomes for 24 months post-graduation.
