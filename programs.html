Our Programmes

Our programmes respond to the realities faced by women, girls, children, youth and vulnerable communities. We use community participation, advocacy, capacity building, service delivery, empowerment, research and partnerships to create sustainable change.

01 · Signature Programme

Rebuilding the Broken Wall

Restoring Families. Rebuilding Lives. Renewing Communities.

Rebuilding the Broken Wall is the signature programme of IWI, designed to restore broken families, strengthen vulnerable individuals and promote peaceful and resilient communities.

It takes a holistic, community-based approach to poverty, conflict, violence, poor health, interrupted education and economic hardship. We work with families, community leaders, women, youth, children and stakeholders to identify challenges and develop lasting solutions.

We also support safe pathways for girls and young women affected by forced marriage, child marriage, abuse or exploitation, including those exposed to survival sex work. Support can include psychosocial care, protection referrals, safe spaces, education, livelihood opportunities and safe reintegration.

Key Interventions
  • Family strengthening and peacebuilding
  • Prevention of child marriage and forced marriage
  • Protection, rescue support and safe reintegration
  • Education and school retention
  • Women's empowerment
  • Youth empowerment
  • Health and wellbeing
  • Protection and safe spaces
  • Community engagement
02

Women's Rights & Empowerment

We support women and girls to understand their rights, participate in decision-making and access opportunities that improve their social and economic wellbeing.

Women's rights awareness · Leadership development · Economic empowerment · Skills development · Advocacy · GBV prevention and referral · Safe spaces · Community engagement.

03

Education & Child Development

We support vulnerable and underserved children through back-to-school initiatives, educational materials, mentorship, life-skills development, child protection awareness, school retention and community awareness.

Every Child Deserves a Chance to Learn, Dream and Thrive.

04

Health & Wellbeing

Our health focus includes reproductive health, maternal and child health, nutrition, WASH, health outreach, psychosocial support, health awareness and community health education.

05

Youth Empowerment

We equip young people through leadership, entrepreneurship, skills development, mentorship, livelihood opportunities, peacebuilding, civic participation and innovation.

06

Food Security & Sustainable Agriculture

We strengthen livelihoods and resilience through climate-smart agriculture, agricultural skills, women and youth participation, livelihood development, food security awareness and community resilience.

07

Male Advocate Engagement

Gender equality requires everyone's participation. IWI engages men and boys as positive advocates and partners in promoting women's rights, preventing violence and building supportive families and communities.

08

Good Governance, Advocacy & Research

We work with communities, government, civil society and other stakeholders through policy advocacy, stakeholder engagement, community mobilization, research, monitoring and evaluation, knowledge sharing and capacity building.
