Our Story

Founded on one belief: proximity changes outcomes.

Imperial Women Initiative began in 2016 as a single literacy circle in a community hall, run by five volunteers who believed that lasting change starts with the person closest to the problem. Today we work across 120 communities — still led by the same principle.

We don't parachute in with fixed solutions. Our field teams live in the communities they serve, our programs are co-designed with the women who use them, and our funding decisions are made with input from local advisory councils. It's slower than a top-down model — and it's the only way we've seen change actually last.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To equip women and girls with education, economic opportunity, health access and leadership training — building the conditions for lasting independence.

Vision

A future where a woman's postcode never determines her potential.
