Who We Are

Imperial Women Initiative (IWI)

Women-Led. Community-Based. Non-Governmental.

Imperial Women Initiative is a women-led, non-governmental and non-religious organization established to promote inclusive and sustainable community development.

IWI was conceived in 2024 following a grassroots initiative focused on expanding access to violence-free spaces for women and girls in rural communities of Bauchi State. The initiative brought together approximately 80 stakeholders, including traditional leaders, government ministries and agencies, health workers, civil society organizations and youth advocates.

Building on this experience, Imperial Women Initiative became a fully registered independent NGO in 2025. Our work has since expanded beyond protection to an integrated approach connecting health, education, economic empowerment, social support, peacebuilding and community development.

Although based in Bauchi State, IWI is committed to developing programmes that can be scaled across Northern Nigeria and other parts of the country.

Our Goal

Inclusive and sustainable community development

To promote inclusive and sustainable community development by empowering individuals through effective programmes, capacity building, policy advocacy and research, with a strong focus on gender equity, food security and resilience.

Our Vision

A flourishing and empowered society

A society where women, girls, children and other vulnerable groups enjoy equal access to opportunities, resources and rights, championed by supportive male advocates for inclusive and sustainable development.

Our Mission

Strengthening community development

To strengthen community development by delivering impactful programmes that empower women, girls, children, men and other vulnerable groups through economic empowerment, access to healthcare, education and social support, while actively promoting violence-free spaces for all.

Strategic Objectives

Five priorities that drive our work

01

Programme Implementation & Project Development

We design and implement programmes addressing social, economic, health and educational needs while monitoring results to promote effectiveness and sustainability.

02

Economic Empowerment & Food Security

We promote women's and youth entrepreneurship, skills development, livelihood support, climate-smart agriculture and sustainable food systems.

03

Capacity Building, Counselling & Training

We strengthen individual and organizational capacity through leadership, vocational skills, mental health, life-skills training and counselling.

04

Policy Advocacy & Influencing

We advocate for gender-sensitive policies and engage government, communities and civil society to promote women's development and social change.

05

Research, Learning & Knowledge Sharing

We conduct participatory research and impact assessments and share evidence, learning and best practices to improve programmes and influence change.
